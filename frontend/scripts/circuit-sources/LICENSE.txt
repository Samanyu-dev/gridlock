These are real, surveyed/traced track outlines (lon/lat LineStrings), not
hand-drawn approximations. build-circuit-asset.mjs (in the parent
directory) projects them to local meters, resamples to an even point
spacing, and detects corners from real curvature — nothing about the
track shape is eyeballed.

No per-point elevation data exists in this source (only a single
altitude figure near start/finish), so every generated circuit's Y is 0.
Flat is the honest state of the data, not a placeholder for a fake
profile.
