Processing performed by GRIDLOCK (frontend/scripts/build-car-asset.mjs):
- renamed nodes/meshes/materials to the GL_Car_* naming convention
- converted materials from KHR_materials_unlit (flat game-asset shading) to
  a standard PBR metallic-roughness workflow, so the showroom's key/fill/
  rim lighting actually affects the car instead of being ignored
- deduplicated + pruned unused data
- produced high/medium/low LOD tiers, medium and low Meshopt-compressed

Status: placeholder ("draft" in the asset manifest). This is a generic
low-poly race car body, not an authentic open-wheel Formula-style
silhouette — it exists so the R3F rendering pipeline (showroom, homepage
hero, driver showroom) can be built and tested against a real, licensed,
correctly-structured GLB. Swap in a proper open-wheel car later by
replacing these three files and bumping the manifest's `version` — no
component code needs to change, since everything reads model paths from
the manifest.
